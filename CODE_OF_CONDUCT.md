# Code of conduct

This project has one maintainer and a small surface, so this is short.

## What is expected

Technical disagreement is welcome and useful. This repository documents a report
of its own that was closed upstream as not-planned, and the maintainer who closed
it was right — that exchange improved the project more than any of its findings
did. Argue with the results, argue with the triage rules, argue with the design.

Not welcome: personal attacks, harassment, or dismissing a person rather than
their argument.

## Reporting findings in other people's projects

A specific clause, because it is the whole point of this tool.

When a scan here produces a hit in someone else's repository, **that project's
maintainers owe you nothing**. Their issue tracker is not a place to publish a
hypothesis and refine it in public. Reproduce it by hand, understand the
mechanism, and file once — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the
Triage section of the [README](README.md).

Filing noise upstream in this project's name is the one thing that would get a
contribution turned away on conduct rather than on merit.

## Reporting a problem

Email [wahibelkhadiri06@gmail.com](mailto:wahibelkhadiri06@gmail.com), or open an
issue if it does not need to be private. Reports go to the maintainer and nobody
else.
